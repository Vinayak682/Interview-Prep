THE ONE STORY YOU'LL REUSE

The two-carrier smartphone case.

A fictional device manufacturer supplies Carrier A and Carrier B. One account faces a demand spike while the other has too much stock. You'll use this case to learn demand planning, forecasting, PSI, stakeholder decisions and inventory reallocation.

THE MOST IMPORTANT PRINCIPLE

Understand → Calculate → Explain → Defend

Build the idea, solve a realistic problem, explain your reasoning out loud, and respond to a difficult follow-up. Behavioral answers must reflect real experience, not invented accomplishments.

MODULE 01 / THE FOUNDATION

Supply chain, from zero.

Understand the ideas before worrying about interview phrasing. Select any concept for a clear meaning and how it appears on the job.

Key distinction: Sell-in versus sell-through

Sell-in means units shipped into the carrier or retail channel. Sell-through means devices purchased by end customers. Strong sell-in with weak sell-through can leave too much inventory in the channel.

KNOWLEDGE CHECK

A phone was shipped to a retailer but has not been sold to a customer. Which event happened?

MODULE 02 / HOW THE WORK GETS DONE

The end-to-end planning process.

Remember D · C · F · A · P · M. This six-step sequence is the backbone of your interview answer.

ROLLING HORIZON

24 weeks, updated every week.

W1–4W5–12W13–24
  • Weeks 1–4: Orders, confirmed arrivals, near-term exceptions.
  • Weeks 5–12: Promotions, supply plans, account conversations.
  • Weeks 13–24: Launches, longer-term capacity and risk scenarios.

These teaching bands are illustrative, not company-specific rules.

SYSTEMS / ERP & AP2

Excel analyzes. Systems operationalize.

An ERP typically records sales orders, purchase orders, receipts, inventory and product data. Planning applications maintain forecast versions and workflows. The company's AP2 process includes forecast review/loading, buffer logic, consensus and reconciliation as described in the supplied job description.

Exact AP2 software behavior and screens are company-specific. Never claim tools you have not used.

“A forecast should reflect expected demand, not simply the sales target.”

Build, adjust, and measure.

ADVANCED EXCEL CHECKLIST

Tick what you can demonstrate.

Excel interview trap

A successful XLOOKUP or VLOOKUP is not proof the value is correct. Always check duplicate keys, inconsistent text/data types, missing mappings, source totals, week alignment and formula fill-down.
